How to fit a datasheet

Choose a template, name your model, and set chart limits to match the datasheet. Enter a representative static operating point and mu, then load a cropped plot image.

Adjust the sliders until the curves match the region relevant to your circuit. This is manual representative fitting, rather than automatic curve digitisation. The pentode fitter displays anode current; its normalisation includes the reference screen current.

Download the JSON when satisfied. Reset template restores its original fields and coefficients while retaining your image.
